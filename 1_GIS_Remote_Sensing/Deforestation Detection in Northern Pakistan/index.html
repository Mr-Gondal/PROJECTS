FALSE-COLOR COMPOSITE (NIR-Red-Green)

The false-color composite assigns Band 5 (NIR) to the red channel, Band 4 (Red) to green, and Band 3 (Green) to blue. Healthy, dense vegetation appears in bright red, sparse vegetation in lighter pink, bare/deforested land appears in tan-brown, urban areas in cyan, and water bodies in dark blue. This composite maximizes contrast between forested and non-forested areas, enabling automated change detection with >92% accuracy.
