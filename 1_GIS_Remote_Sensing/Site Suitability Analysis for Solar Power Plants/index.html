02

MCE Methodology & Workflow

🛰
Data Acquisition
NASA POWER GHI, SRTM DEM (slope), ESA WorldCover 2021, OSM Roads & Grid lines
🔧
Pre-processing
Raster resampling to 250m, projection to UTM 42N, cloud masking, gap-fill
📊
Normalization
Linear min-max normalization per criterion; inverse distance for constraints
⚖️
Weighted Overlay
Weighted Linear Combination (WLC): S = Σ wᵢ · xᵢ for each pixel
🗺
Suitability Map
4-class output: Highly Suitable, Suitable, Marginal, Not Suitable
📍
Site Selection
Top contiguous patches by area ranked; exclusion zones applied (urban, protected, water)

Energy Potential Calculator

Enter site parameters to calculate annual energy output, CO₂ offset, homes powered, and estimated revenue.

Formula:
E = GHI × Area × η × PR
CO₂ = E × 0.433 kg/kWh
Homes = E / 4.5 MWh/yr
Revenue = E × PKR 15/kWh
