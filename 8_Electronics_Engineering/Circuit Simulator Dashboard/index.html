Engineering Notes

In a series RC circuit, total impedance combines resistance and capacitive reactance. The current leads the voltage because the capacitor charges before the resistor voltage reaches its peak.

Xc = 1 / (2*pi*f*C), Z = sqrt(R^2 + Xc^2), phi = -atan(Xc / R)
